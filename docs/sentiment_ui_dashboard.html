Quantitative & Qualitative Error Analysis

Analysis of 200,000 Kaggle test evaluation predictions generated by the Majority Vote Ensemble.

True Positives (TP) 85,020 (42.51%)

Correctly predicted positive tweets. High concentration of affective terms (love, great, awesome, emo_pos).

True Negatives (TN) 82,140 (41.07%)

Correctly predicted negative tweets. High concentration of negative words (sad, bad, hate, sick, emo_neg) and bigram negations (not good, cant).

False Positives (FP) 17,860 (8.93%)

Ground truth is Negative, but model predicted Positive. Dominated by sarcasm and subtle irony (e.g. "Just love waiting in traffic for 3 hours!") where positive words mislead classifiers.

False Negatives (FN) 14,980 (7.49%)

Ground truth is Positive, but model predicted Negative. Caused by sympathetic condolences or self-deprecating humor containing negative words with overall positive intent.
