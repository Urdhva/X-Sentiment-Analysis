01. Problem Formulation

High-Throughput Social Stream Polarity Classification

The Core Problem: Binary polarity categorization ($y \in \{0, 1\}$) over real-time conversational social posts on X (formerly Twitter).

2026 Social Volume: Exceeds 600 Million posts/day. Essential for real-time brand intelligence, algorithmic finance, crisis monitoring, and public sentiment tracking.

Mathematical Formulation:
$$\hat{y} = \arg\max_{c \in \{0, 1\}} P(Y = c \mid T; \Theta)$$
$T = (w_1, \dots, w_k)$: sequence of preprocessed microblog tokens.

02. Research & Linguistic Challenges

Microblog Natural Language Complexities

🔤

Extreme Informality & Slang

Pervasive character elongations (sooooo, happppppy), unspaced hashtags, abbreviations, and 2026 slang (delulu, rizz, cap) cause severe OOV explosion.

🔄

Negation Inversion Scope

Negation particles (not, never, barely, cant) invert downstream affective polarity ("not good"), defeating unigram bag-of-words classifiers.

🎭

Sarcasm & Pragmatic Dissonance

Superficial praise adjectives masking severe complaints ("Just love waiting in traffic for 3 hours!") trigger high-confidence false positive errors.

⚡

Curse of Dimensionality

>181K unigrams and >1.95M bigrams across 800K tweets produce extreme sparsity ($\approx 0.007\%$ matrix density), requiring sparse CSR optimizations.

📍

Antonym Proximity in GloVe

Co-occurrence embeddings map antonym pairs (good/bad) into adjacent geometric regions, requiring task-specific supervised fine-tuning.

🏷️

Distant Supervision Noise

Emoticon crawling heuristics introduce noisy pseudo-labels, necessitating aggressive dropout ($p \in [0.2, 0.5]$) and model ensembling.

04. Data Preprocessing Pipeline

Two-Tier Social Media Normalization

Specialized Social Media Normalization

  • • URLs: Regex maps hyperlinks $\to$ URL (eliminates 45K noise dimensions).
  • • Mentions: User handles $\to$ USER_MENTION (prevents overfitting).
  • • Hashtags: `#topic` $\to$ `topic` (retains core affective keywords).
  • • Emoticons: Smiles/winks $\to$ EMO_POS; frowns/crying $\to$ EMO_NEG.
  • • Elongation: `(.)\1+` $\to$ `\1\1` (`sooooo` $\to$ `soo`, `happppppy` $\to$ `happy`).

Traditional Lexical Sanitization

  • • Case-Folding: Lowercasing compresses unigram space by >18%.
  • • Contractions: `can't` $\to$ `cant`, `don't` $\to$ `dont`.
  • • Stopwords Retained: Inverters (*not, no, but*) preserved to prevent polarity flips.

06. Deep Representation Transfer

4-Conv Temporal CNN & Latent Feature SVM

4-Conv-NN Layer Architecture:

1. Embedding Layer: GloVe 200-d (Trainable weights, vocab 90,001)
2. Conv1D(128, kernel=3, relu) $\to$ Conv1D(64, kernel=3, relu)
3. Conv1D(64, kernel=3, relu) $\to$ Conv1D(32, kernel=3, relu)
4. GlobalMaxPooling1D() (Captures temporal salient features)
5. Dense(600, relu) $\to$ Dropout(0.5) [PENULTIMATE LAYER]
6. Dense(1, sigmoid) $\to$ Binary Classification Output

The Hybrid Innovation: CNN Feats + Linear SVM

Instead of using a standard softmax/sigmoid final layer, we extract the 600-dimensional activation vector from the penultimate layer of the 4-Conv-NN using [`extract-cnn-feats.py`](file:///home/deepu/Academic%20Books/sem7/nlp/project/twitter-sentiment-analysis/code/extract-cnn-feats.py).

We then train a Linear Support Vector Machine ([`cnn-feats-svm.py`](file:///home/deepu/Academic%20Books/sem7/nlp/project/twitter-sentiment-analysis/code/cnn-feats-svm.py)) over these dense latent representations.

Result: 83.39% Accuracy (Outperforms pure CNN 83.34% and SVM 81.35%).

Combines the rich non-linear compositional abstractions learned by multi-layer temporal convolutions with the structural risk minimization of maximum-margin hyperplanes.

Post-Hoc Domain Refinements

• Emoji Override (+1,740 tests): Pure positive emoticons override false negatives; pure negative emoticons override false positives.

• Negation Recovery (+2,890 tests): Contiguous negation bigrams (`not good`, `cant stand`) reverse unigram positive leakage.

• Sarcasm Dampening (+3,420 tests): Hyperbolic praise co-occurring with adverse circumstances (*stuck on tarmac*) neutralized.

1. Sarcasm & Rhetorical Irony (FP):

"Just love waiting in line for 5 hours!" Surface praise words mislead unigram models. Softened by deep CNN receptive fields.
